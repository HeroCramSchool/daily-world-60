# Daily World 60 — Research Brief (2026)

Channel: @60dailyworld. 60-second daily world-news Shorts / Reels / TikTok.
Goal: feed prompts directly into image + video generation. All specs concrete.

## 1. YouTube Shorts Thumbnail (player 1080x1920 + Search/Browse 1280x720)

YouTube renders the Shorts thumbnail in two contexts. The vertical (1080x1920) shows in the Shorts player and Subscriptions feed; the 16:9 crop shows in Search, Home, and channel pages — so design the 1080x1920 with the center safe-crop matching a 1280x720 frame at the middle. PixExact and Alici.AI confirm both renderings are live in 2026 (Alici.AI, "YouTube Thumbnail Size 2026", Mar 2026).

**Face vs. no-face for faceless channels.** Face advantage is overstated below ~200K subs and varies by niche. AutoFaceless's 2026 stats study (Mar 2026) found face vs. no-face thumbnails perform statistically similar for faceless channels; faces only meaningfully boost CTR above 200K subs. **For a 0-sub launch, ditch the face requirement and lean on object + bold word.**

**Text length.** 0–3 words wins. ThumbMagic's 2026 CTR benchmarks report (Apr 2026) and BananaThumbnail (Feb 2026) cite eye-tracking studies showing >5 words creates cognitive friction and drops CTR. For news: a single noun + an emotive modifier (e.g., "ロシア急展開", "イラン崩壊か") outperforms full sentences.

**Color/contrast.** Complementary pairs at max separation: red #CC0000 (CNN/BBC news red — signals urgency, Logos-World 2026) on near-black #0A0A0A, or yellow #FFD400 on #1A1A1A. Contrast ratio ≥7:1. Avoid muddy mid-tones — they vanish at 120px mobile feed render (WebFX, "Mobile dominance now 63% of watch time", Mar 2026).

**Emotional trigger for news/world niche.** Curiosity gap + locator. Use a **map outline**, a **flag fragment**, or a **single object** (missile silhouette, podium, currency note) at 60% scale, top-2/3 of frame. ThumbMagic (Apr 2026) — "this happened" style commentary thumbnails outperform pure portrait shots in news.

**What drops CTR in news in 2026** (BananaThumbnail "7 Mistakes Killing CTR", Feb 2026; Miraflow "Why Your Thumbnail Is Killing CTR", Apr 2026):
- Generic stock-photo composition (centered subject + plain backdrop).
- Hyper-polished AI faces (plastic skin) — algorithmically demoted post Neal Mohan authenticity letter (late 2025) and Q1 2026 model update.
- Over-saturated, gradient-heavy backdrops (read as AI slop).
- News-channel logo mimicry — viewers flag as ragebait, watch-then-bounce kills retention.

**Font weight.** ExtraBold / Black weight (700–900). Avoid Helvetica default — use **Anton, Bebas Neue, Noto Sans JP Black, BIZ UDPGothic Bold**. White text, 8–12px black stroke outline + 4px hard drop shadow.

## 2. Instagram Reels Cover (1080x1920) + Feed Post (1080x1080)

Reels cover shows at ~360x640 in grid view, so design must read at thumb size.

**Safe zones (Reels 1080x1920)** (TryMyPost "Safe Zones 2026", Mar 2026; Kreatli 2026 guide):
- Top buffer: 210px (username/audio bar).
- Bottom buffer: 310–320px (caption + action stack).
- Right buffer: 84–120px (like/comment/share icons).
- **Hook text Y-range: 200px–600px from top, centered horizontally.** This is universally safe across notches and Dynamic Island.

**Feed post 1080x1080**: full canvas usable, but Metricool's 2026 guide notes the bottom 40px gets clipped on Reels-as-grid views — keep margin.

**News/daily-recap fonts.** Bold sans-serif. Cover minimum 60px at 1080 width per Metricool (2026). Recommended: **Montserrat Black, Inter ExtraBold, Noto Sans JP Black**. Cueball Creatives (2026) — high-contrast white-on-dark or black-on-neon "sticker" backgrounds outperform photo-overlay styles for news.

**Hook line patterns that work** (Predis.ai 2026, TryMyPost 2026):
- Date-stamped recap: "5/30 世界の3本" — feels like a daily newspaper.
- Number + curiosity gap: "今日の3ニュース".
- Stakes statement: "知らないとマズい".
Avoid "今日のニュースまとめ" generic — too soft.

**Color combos that trend in 2026 news content.** (Pinterest 2026 boards + Predis.ai):
- Deep navy #0F1B3D + acid yellow #F5E63B (Bloomberg-coded, signals serious).
- Charcoal #1A1A1A + signal red #E63946 (BBC-coded, urgency).
- Off-white #F4F1EA + ink #0A0A0A + single accent #C9A227 (newspaper-coded, anti-AI feel).
Avoid: pastel gradients, purple-pink Instagram-default palette, golden-hued AI defaults (aigoodies 2026 — flagged as AI-slop tells).

**Face element.** Predis.ai 2026 — face with clear emotion = +38% engagement, but for a faceless news brand substitute a **single high-emotion object** (broken glass, falling stocks chart, megaphone silhouette).

## 3. TikTok Cover (1080x1920) + First-3-Second Hook

**Cover (1080x1920).** TikTok cover competes on the FYP profile grid and search. Same safe-zone logic as Reels but bottom UI is shallower (~200px vs IG's 320). Per Vugola (2026) and Blitzcut (Apr 2026): high-contrast text top-third, emotive visual middle, leave bottom 200px clean.

**First 3 seconds — stop-the-scroll patterns** (Sprout Social "TikTok Algorithm 2026", May 2026; Buffer 2026; Opus.pro 2026):
- Actual window is **1.5 seconds**, not 3 — first frame and first words decide everything.
- Algorithm 2026 prioritizes "durable attention" — watch-time, completion rate (target 70%+), rewatches above likes/comments.
- Strong hook formulas:
  1. **Question demanding answer** — "なぜ今、円が140円台?"
  2. **Surprising stat** — "1日でAppleが30兆円消えた"
  3. **Visual transformation/reveal** — map zooms, currency shifts.
  4. **Pattern interrupt** — first frame is the unexpected object, not the speaker.
  5. **Direct address** — "投資してる人だけ見て"

**Edutainment formula wins news/educational** (Sprout Social 2026): the most consistent path to virality is value (education) wrapped in entertainment pace. Pure recap = flat; recap with a "why this matters to YOU" beat = viral.

**What TikTok 2026 algorithm penalizes** (Socialync May 2026, Opus.pro 2026): low-effort reposts, mass-produced AI voice + stock B-roll combos, generic FYP-bait hashtags. Original niche content + audio still wins.

## 4. 60-Second Vertical News Composition

**Story budget for 3 stories + intro + outro = 60s.**
- Intro hook: 3s.
- Story 1: 16s.
- Story 2: 16s.
- Story 3: 16s.
- Outro / CTA: 9s.
Total: 60s. (Joyspace 2026 data study — clips 15–45s deliver highest engagement; 60s OK if pacing is tight.)

**Cuts/scene per story.** Per Specificit (2026) and Visla Trends (2026): vertical news needs visual change every 1.5–2.5s to prevent drop-off. For each 16s story:
- 0–2s: headline card (big text on solid color).
- 2–6s: B-roll #1 (map/photo/object).
- 6–10s: B-roll #2 (different angle — chart, second photo, archive clip).
- 10–14s: VO continues + animated stat overlay.
- 14–16s: takeaway line full-screen.
That's **5 cuts per 16s** = ~3 cuts/sec average across the full 60s = **~30 total cuts**.

**Subtitle style — what hits hardest in 2026.**
Word-by-word (karaoke pop) wins for retention. Confirmed across Blitzcut "Best Caption Style TikTok 2026" (Apr 2026), Vocallab "Word-Level Timestamped Subtitles 2026" (Mar 2026), Braiv (2026), Opus.pro (2026):
- Style: word-by-word with current-word highlight color shift.
- Base color: white #FFFFFF.
- Active word: yellow #FFD400 (high-energy) or signal red #E63946 (urgency/breaking).
- Outline: black #000000, stroke 6–10px, hard (no blur).
- Optional 3–5px hard drop shadow #000000 at 70% opacity.

**Font + size for 1080x1920.**
- Primary caption font size: **72pt (≈96px)** — Vocallab/Opus.pro 2026 range is 55–75pt; news brand should land at top of range for grandparent-readable. Min 48px per Legibility.info 2026.
- Font: **Noto Sans JP Black** or **BIZ UDPGothic Bold** (Japanese); **Inter ExtraBold** or **Anton** for any English headers.
- Max 30 chars/line (Latin), 14 chars/line (Japanese). Max 2 lines on screen.
- Y-position: 1100–1300px (lower-middle third, above the bottom 280–320px UI strip).

**Headline cards** between stories: 110–140pt font, single solid background (#0F1B3D or #1A1A1A), 0.4s flash-in, full-frame.

## 5. Anti-AI-Slop Red Flags (Specific Things to Avoid)

What reviewers/editors flag as AI-generated in 2026 (Charlie Guo "Field Guide to AI Slop", Apr 2026; Creative Boom "10 trends creatives are over in 2026", Mar 2026; Colecandoo Feb 2026; aigoodies "Aesthetics 2026", Apr 2026; Delivered Social 2026; TinyTechGuides 2026):

**Visual cues to actively avoid:**
- **Gemini-default gradients** — blue→purple→pink soft fades. Reads as AI-generated instantly.
- **Golden-hour overlay** — AI tools default to honey-gold lighting on everything (aigoodies Apr 2026).
- **Hyper-polished plastic skin** — Q1 2026 YouTube model demoted these. Use real texture, grain, or remove faces.
- **Mathematical noise patterns** — AI images have uniform "star-shaped" noise vs. real photos' chaotic grain. Add **3–5% film grain** in post.
- **Default Helvetica / Arial / Roboto Regular** — instant AI flag. Use deliberate display weight (Anton, Bebas Neue, Noto Sans JP Black).
- **Symmetrical, centered everything** — break composition. Off-center hero element at 1/3 line.
- **Stock-photo "businessperson points at chart"** type B-roll — algorithmically demoted.
- **Em-dash (—) and "It's not X, it's Y" phrasing** in on-screen text — Field Guide 2026 confirms still a strong tell.
- **Emoji-led bullet lists** with checkmarks + light-bulbs (TinyTechGuides 2026).

**Audio/voice signals AI detectors catch:**
- Unnatural cadence at end of sentences (no breath, no hesitation).
- Same VO pacing across every story — humans vary by topic emotion.
- Add 1 deliberate human marker: a "ま、" filler, a swallow, a real pause >0.8s.

**Composition signals that read as human:**
- One asymmetric element per frame.
- Type with a *story* (not template) — date stamp, dateline ("Tokyo / 5月30日"), real source watermark.
- Mix of font weights (ExtraBold headline + Regular dateline) — AI templates use one weight.
- Visible "imperfections" — slight tilt on B-roll inserts, hand-style chevron arrows over geometric ones.

**Net rule:** if the design could come out of Canva's default template in 5 clicks, it reads as slop. Add one deliberately human edit per frame.
